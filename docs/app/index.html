哪些孩子适合参加？Who is the beta for?

目前重点招募在 BC 读四至七年级、家里有 iPhone 或 iPad，并愿意实际使用后给反馈的家庭。We are looking for BC families with a student in Grades 4–7, an iPhone or iPad, and time to share honest feedback.

怎么安装？How do I install it?

点击 TestFlight 按钮，在设备上安装 Apple 的 TestFlight，然后接受圆圆老师的测试邀请即可。Tap the TestFlight button, install Apple's TestFlight app, and accept the Yuanyuan Math invitation.

测试收费吗？Does the beta cost anything?

首轮 TestFlight 测试免费。我们更需要的是孩子真实使用后的反馈。The first TestFlight beta is free. What matters most is feedback after real use.

怎么反馈问题？How do I send feedback?
